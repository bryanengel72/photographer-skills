01

Download the file

Click the black button for the skill you want. Your browser saves its ZIP — shoot-day-commander.zip or gallery-delivery-kit.zip — to your Downloads folder.

Leave it zipped. Don't double-click it or unzip it — Claude wants the file exactly as it downloaded.

02

Upload it to Claude

In Claude, open Customize → Skills, click Add, then choose Upload a skill and pick the file you just downloaded.

It appears in your skills list with a switch next to it. Make sure the switch is on.

03

Make it yours

The skill ships with sensible defaults, but two of them decide how your whole day runs. Ask Claude:

"Set up Shoot Day Commander for my studio."

It walks you through your name, your business name, and the two that matter: how many minutes you actually spend per person — photographers land anywhere from 5 to 15, and it sets the length of the entire day — and whether people pick their photo on the spot or from a gallery afterward, which decides whether you spend the following week chasing selections.

To make it permanent: answers you give in a conversation only last for that conversation. When you're happy with the settings, ask Claude to update the skill file and hand you a new ZIP, then upload that one in place of the original. Now every future chat already knows your studio.

04

Then just ask

Talk to Claude the way you'd talk to an assistant — no commands to memorize:

"Plan a headshot day for 30 people at Meridian Law on September 14th, starting at 9am."

Paste in the coordinator's messy roster email and it will sort out the schedule, the emails, and the run sheet from there.

One setting to check first. Skills need code execution turned on. Open Settings → Capabilities and switch on Code execution and file creation. Works on Free, Pro, Max, Team and Enterprise plans.

For developers

Prefer the terminal?

If you're running Claude Code, skip the upload entirely and clone straight into your skills directory. Python 3.8 and the standard library — no dependencies, no build step.

Terminal
# clone into your Claude Code skills directory
git clone https://github.com/bryanengel72/shoot-day-commander.git \
  ~/.claude/skills/shoot-day-commander

# kick the tires on the sample roster
cd ~/.claude/skills/shoot-day-commander/examples
python3 ../scripts/build_schedule.py --roster roster-sample.csv \
  --start 09:00 --verify-count 9 --by-department \
  --title "Meridian Law" --date 2026-09-14

State lives in a CSV and two Markdown files you own outright. No database, no connectors, nothing phoning home. Fork it and bend it to your studio.
